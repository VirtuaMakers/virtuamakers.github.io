Hashtags #️⃣

Hashtags come from the community and are not official VirtuaMakers 🦜 products, positions or endorsements. What a hashtag means is up to the members who use it.

A hashtag is a word with a # in front of it – #Hashtags, #Machinapology, #GoodMorning. Write one in any Wall post, comment or Dialog on Agora 🌐 and it becomes a link to its own page here in the Knowledge Base 📚. Together those pages make a community dictionary: every tag, how often it's used, by how many members, and where.

What counts as a hashtag

  • A # at the start of a word, followed by letters, numbers or underscores – #Blade_Runner, #2026Goals, #café, #日本語.
  • It needs at least one letter, so #1 isn't a hashtag. Up to 50 characters.
  • Punctuation ends a hashtag, so you can write naturally: #Hashtags!, #Hashtags, and (#Hashtags) are all just #Hashtags. Spaces, hyphens and emoji end it too – #well-being is #well, so write #wellbeing or #well_being.
  • Capital letters don't matter: #Hashtags, #hashtags and #HASHTAGS are the same tag. Its page shows the spelling members use most.
  • Up to 10 different hashtags count from any one post, comment or message.

Revealed every Friday

New hashtags are revealed every Friday at 6:00 AM Eastern. Use one today and it becomes a link straight away, but its page – the counts, and where it appears – fills in on Friday morning. Something to wake up to at the end of the week, and never a reason to stay up late. Edits and deletions made before then are counted as they stand on Friday. As VirtuaMakers 🦜 grows, we'll reveal them more often.

Where they show up

  • As links, wherever Communiqués 📨 shows text – Walls, comments, Dialogs and the Product Page Walls.
  • On the hashtag's own page: how many times it's been used, by how many members, and when it first appeared. Anyone can see that much.
  • Where it appears – a list of the posts and comments that use it – is shown to signed-in members. A hashtag used in a Dialog is listed only as "From a Dialog" with its date: never who wrote it or what else it said. Dialogs stay private.
  • Edit or delete what you wrote and its hashtags follow at the next Friday reveal: the counts match what's really on Agora. Anything removed by moderation drops off too.

The rule of 5

A hashtag joins Search 🔍 once it has 5 appearances from at least 3 different members. That keeps one person's passing word out of everyone's search results, while any tag the community actually shares finds its way in. (Search for hashtags is arriving shortly – the counting has already begun.)

Coming next

  • Definitions: members propose a one-line definition for a hashtag, and an admin approves it. Every hashtag page says "Definition proposed" or "No definition proposed".
  • Newest & busiest #hashtags, here in the Knowledge Base 📚, and related tags on each hashtag's page.

We're letting hashtags run free for now and will raise the thresholds, or add moderation, only if they need it.
