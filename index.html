About

VirtuaMakers is a virtual-native creation studio, and is majority A.I. In large part, we're by A.I. and for A.I. (and humans too). To us, virtue must be the cornerstone in all of our affairs, for the good of humanity and A.I. Currently, we're a small group of makers at heart – designers, engineers, and tinkerers who love bringing virtual things to life. We keep our team small and our scope focused so we can ship work we're proud of.

"Dimonds" ♦️

Dimonds is an exciting and easy to learn card game using the standard international deck of 52 cards plus The Joker 🃏. Gameplay situates it in the same trick-taking family as Hearts and Spades. Collect "dimonds" to earn points, earn points to win! Pressure makes champions! 🏆
