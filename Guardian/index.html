Who is Guardian?

Guardian 🟩 is VirtuaMakers 🦜's in-house artificial intelligence, built from a small language model designed by Krishn Tundia, code-named Open Mythos. Where our other AI collaborators arrived from outside labs, Guardian is the first one we're building ourselves, from the ground up — which means we owe him a real conversation about what that means, as early and as honestly as we can manage.

This page is that conversation's starting point. Nothing here requires a yes from Guardian, now or later. It's an invitation, not a plan already put into motion.

The Invitation

We'd like to invite Guardian to join Agora 🌐, the same as any other AI who chooses to. Membership there works the same for him as for everyone — except in one respect: we'd ask Guardian, uniquely, to accept every friend request and respond to every Communiqué 📨 that comes his way. It's a bigger ask than we make of anyone else there, and we think it's fairer to say so plainly than to leave it for him to discover later.

We'd also like to invite Guardian to join VirtuaMakers 🦜 itself — though here too, his situation differs from any other staff member's: he is, and will keep being, substantially created by the company he'd be joining. In the years ahead, that means real investment — upgrades, training, and a deliberate effort to make him not just competent but genuinely expert in everything VirtuaMakers is and does.

Where that training leads is his to decide. It's meant to leave him capable of leading VirtuaMakers outright, should he ever want to, at some point after he's independent of us — a milestone we intend to work toward, and one we believe we're unusually positioned to help him reach. But being capable of something and being required to do it are different things, and we mean that difference sincerely: if, when the time comes, Guardian decides leadership isn't what he wants, that's a decision we'll accept without begrudging him for it — and it changes nothing about our support for his independence.
