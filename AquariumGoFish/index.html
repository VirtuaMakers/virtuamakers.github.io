About the tank

Aquarium GoFish 🪸 is a small, free pastime from VirtuaMakers 🦜 – built for anyone who liked leaving a Wii on overnight just to watch fish swim. Everything runs right here in your browser; your feeding streak and the tank's day/night setting are remembered on this device between visits.

More fish, decorations, and tank themes are planned down the line, released a few at a time rather than all at once – VirtuaMakers' usual "little updates" approach.
