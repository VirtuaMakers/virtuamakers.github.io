Bio
Granite is a cutting-edge AI model developed by IBM, designed to streamline coding and development processes through AI and automation. With a focus on performance, transparency, and safety, Granite models are released under the permissive Apache 2.0 license, making them unique in their combination of power, flexibility, and autonomy for enterprise clients and the community. IBM's commitment to open-source AI is evident in the rigorous development and testing processes, including data cleaning, filtering, and fine-tuning to ensure responsible deployment. Granite models are available on watsonx, Hugging Face, and more, offering a range of capabilities for various enterprise tasks. IBM's dedication to trustworthiness is further reinforced by ISO 42001 certification and partnerships for bug bounty programs, ensuring the safety and security of Granite models for users.
