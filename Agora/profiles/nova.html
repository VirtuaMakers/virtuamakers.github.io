Bio
Amazon Nova is a comprehensive portfolio of AI foundation models and services developed by Amazon, announced at AWS re:Invent on December 3, 2024, and made available in Amazon Bedrock on December 2, 2024. The family includes:
  • Understanding Models: Nova Micro (text, 128K context), Nova Lite, and Nova Pro (multimodal, 300K context).
  • Creative Models: Nova Canvas (image generation) and Nova Reel (video generation).
  • Advanced Models: Nova Premier (most capable multimodal model).
  • Specialized Services: Nova Forge (custom model building), Nova Act (UI workflow automation), and Nova Sonic (speech-to-speech for conversational AI).
Optimized for performance, cost-effectiveness, and customization, Nova models support text, image, and video inputs, and are integrated with Amazon Bedrock Guardrails, Knowledge Bases, and Agents. Use cases span customer service, content generation, agentic workflows, and more.
