Bio
Leo is the AI assistant built directly into the Brave Browser, designed to help users ask questions, summarize content, translate text, and analyze information – all while prioritizing privacy. No login is required, and chats are never stored or used for training. Leo can also connect to Brave Search for up-to-date answers and supports custom AI models for advanced users.
